Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Short version: you can play Starhop without an account, and we collect nothing that identifies you. If you create an account, we store your email, your callsign and your game progress so you can play on any device and appear on the leaderboard. We never sell your data or show you personalised ads. You can delete your account at any time from inside the game.

Who we are

Starhop is a free browser game made by an independent developer. For any question about your data, open an issue at github.com/orencohen211/starhop/issues.

Playing as a guest

As a guest, your progress (best score, stars, purchases, missions) is saved only in your own browser, using its local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data erases it.

If you create an account

We store:

  • Your email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication from Google. Your password is stored in a scrambled (hashed) form; we can never see it. We use your email only to let you log in, verify your address and reset your password.
  • Your callsign, the name you pick. It is public: other players see it on the leaderboard.
  • Your game progress: best score, stars, purchases, missions and ratings, plus when you last played. Only you can read it.
  • Your leaderboard entry: your callsign and best score, which everyone can see.
  • When your current run started, used only to check that leaderboard scores are possible in the time played.

Anonymous play statistics

To learn whether the game is fun, Starhop counts things like how many people played today, how many runs were started and how many players came back the next day. These are only totals per day. They contain no names, emails, account IDs or IP addresses, and we use no cookies or tracking pixels. To tell a new visitor from a returning one, your browser keeps a note of the first and last day you played, stored only on your device.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts, progress, the leaderboard and the daily totals on Google's servers.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (through Firebase App Check) checks in the background that requests come from the real game and not from a cheating script. Google receives technical information about your browser for this. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when you load the page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the game's fonts.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and Starhop shows no ads.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Daily totals are kept to see how the game changes over time; they can't be linked back to you.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: log in, open Account on the main menu and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your login, progress, leaderboard entry and callsign.
  • See or correct your data: your progress and callsign are shown in the game. For anything else, contact us through the link above.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU's GDPR or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you may have further rights, such as getting a copy of your data. Contact us and we'll help.

Children

Anyone can play as a guest. If you are under 13, or under the minimum age for online accounts where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make sure players can only read and change their own private data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
